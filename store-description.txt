YouTube Tab-Fullscreen

Expand YouTube videos to fill your entire browser tab, similar to Twitch's theater mode.

What It Does

This extension adds a tab-fullscreen mode to YouTube. Unlike regular fullscreen, the video stays within your browser tab so you can still see other tabs and use browser shortcuts.

Features

- Expand videos beyond standard theater mode to fill your tab
- Press Alt+T or click the toolbar button to toggle
- Remembers your preference as you browse videos
- Matches YouTube's native design
- Lightweight (under 50KB)

How to Use

1. Install the extension
2. Go to any YouTube video
3. Click the button in video controls or press Alt+T

Privacy

- No data collection or tracking
- No external connections
- Only requires activeTab permission

Compatibility

- Works on all YouTube video pages
- Supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Firefox
- Compatible with YouTube Premium and other extensions
